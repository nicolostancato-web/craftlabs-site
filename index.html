01 — What we run

Four systems. One job: close the position clean.

ATOMIC

Atomic by design

Every operation completes inside a single transaction, or not at all. No positions carried, no exposure left behind when the block closes.

RESILIENT

Built for volatility

Our systems are designed for the worst minutes of the market — when prices gap, liquidity thins and everything happens at once.

PRIVATE

Private execution

Our orders never touch the public mempool. Nothing to copy, nothing to front-run.

LIQUIDITY

Execution at size

We move meaningful size in volatile conditions, and we are building market-maker partnerships to do it with minimal market impact.

04 — Principles

Rules the code enforces. Not promises.

/01

All or nothing

Every operation is atomic. A failed sale or an unprofitable fill is a revert, never a loss.

/02

No user funds

We never hold third-party deposits. Our contracts only touch assets inside the liquidation itself.

/03

Simulate first

Every transaction is simulated against its target state before a single byte is sent.
