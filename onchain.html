Liquidation infrastructure that settles in a single transaction.

We build and operate the contracts and off-chain systems that keep Aave v3 positions healthy: unhealthy debt is repaid, collateral is sold and the loan is closed atomically, or nothing happens at all.

What we run

Atomic liquidations

Flash-funded repayment, collateral seizure and collateral sale inside one transaction. If any step fails, the whole transaction reverts.

Batched execution

Several positions that become liquidatable in the same block are handled together in one transaction, with per-position isolation.

Private order flow

Bundles are submitted privately through Flashbots and MEV-Share, including the Chainlink SVR oracle-update auctions.

Best-execution routing

Collateral is sold across on-chain venues (Uniswap v3 and v4, Curve, aggregators), and we are integrating RFQ market-maker liquidity for large sizes.

How a liquidation settles

  1. DetectWe track health factors and oracle updates, and simulate every candidate against the state it will execute on.
  2. BorrowThe debt asset is flash-borrowed for the length of the transaction.
  3. LiquidateThe position is liquidated on the Aave v3 pool and the collateral is received by our contract.
  4. SellThe collateral is sold for the debt asset; sale amounts are set from the balance actually received.
  5. Repay or revertThe flash loan is repaid and a minimum profit is enforced on-chain. Otherwise the transaction reverts and nothing settles.

Principles

All or nothing

Every operation is atomic. A failed sale or an unprofitable fill means a revert, not a loss.

No user funds

We never hold third-party deposits. Contracts only handle assets inside the liquidation itself.

Simulate first

Every transaction is simulated against the target state before it is submitted.
